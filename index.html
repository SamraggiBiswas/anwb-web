What a dynamic tariff means for your bill

You pay the hourly market price plus purchasing cost and taxes. Prices are typically lowest at night and around midday, highest between 17:00 and 20:00. Households that shift the dishwasher, washing machine and EV charging save on average € 18 a month compared with a fixed contract.

Not sure? Many members start dynamic and switch to fixed later without a penalty.
